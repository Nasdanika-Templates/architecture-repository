[#architecture]
= Sample Architecture

[partintro]
--
This part is what an AI assistant drafted from the sample prompt in <<sample-prompt>>, reviewed
and corrected by people, and then organized along the phases of the TOGAF ADM. The context is the
{system} of the C4 model's well-known example, extended with a new capability, an {feature},
built on Azure OpenAI.

It is a sample: replace it with your own architecture. It shows the shape of the result, not
the answer for your organization. The ranking of alternatives in <<alternatives>> is a draft for the
people who decide, and most of the decisions in <<decision-log>> are, accordingly, not yet
[.decided]#DECIDED#.
--
