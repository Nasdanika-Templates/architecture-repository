[#decisions-method]
= Decisions, Assumptions and Open Questions

Three registers keep the state of the architecture honest: the decision log, the assumptions and
the open questions. The assistant maintains them; people own every entry.

[#adr]
== Architecture Decision Records

Each significant decision has an Architecture Decision Record (ADR): a short file in
`src/docs/asciidoc/architecture/decisions/`, numbered, never renumbered and never deleted. A decision
that changes is superseded by a new ADR, which links back.

An ADR has these sections:

[horizontal]
Status:: One of the statuses below, with the date it was reached and who reached it.
Context:: What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, the relevant facts and
assumptions, by reference.
Alternatives:: The options considered, including doing nothing, with their scores when they were
ranked (<<alternatives>>).
Decision:: The choice, stated in one or two sentences.
Consequences:: What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what follows: new work, new risks, new
assumptions.
Related:: Assumptions, open questions, meetings and other ADRs.

The ADR format is Michael Nygard's, with the alternatives made explicit, because a ranking of
alternatives against criteria is where an assistant helps most and where a reviewer most needs to
see the reasoning.

[#decision-statuses]
== Decision Statuses

[cols="1,3,2"]
|===
|Status |Meaning |Moves to

|[.proposed]#PROPOSED#
|Drafted, often by the assistant, with alternatives and a recommendation. Nobody has decided yet.
|PENDING, DECIDED, REJECTED

|[.pending]#PENDING#
|Reviewed, and waiting for something specific: an evaluation, a piece of information, a meeting.
The ADR names what it waits for, usually an open question, and who will decide.
|DECIDED, REJECTED, DEFERRED

|[.decided]#DECIDED#
|Decided by the named owner on the given date. Changing it takes a new ADR.
|SUPERSEDED

|[.deferred]#DEFERRED#
|Consciously postponed, with the trigger for revisiting it, such as a phase, a date or an event.
|PROPOSED

|[.rejected]#REJECTED#
|Considered and declined. Kept, so the question is not reopened without new information.
|-

|[.superseded]#SUPERSEDED#
|Replaced by a later ADR, which it names.
|-
|===

Only a person moves a decision to DECIDED. The assistant may propose a status change, for example
when a meeting note records a decision, and the reviewer of the diff confirms it.

[#registers]
== Assumptions and Open Questions

Assumptions (<<assumptions>>) have an ID, a statement, an owner, a way to validate it, a date by
which it must be validated, and a status: open, validated or invalidated. An invalidated assumption
is a signal: ask the assistant which decisions and sections depend on it.

Open questions (<<open-questions>>) have an ID, the question, an owner, a due date and, when
answered, the answer and its source. Most PENDING decisions wait for an open question.

Keep the registers as AsciiDoc tables while they are small. When they grow, or when they must be
shared with tools, keep them as data, for example in YAML or CSV, and generate the tables in the
build (<<models>>); AsciiDoc tables can include CSV directly.

[#meeting-notes-method]
== Meeting Notes

Meeting notes (<<meeting-notes>>) are the evidence. Each note has the date, the participants by
role, a summary, and the facts, assumptions, decisions, open questions and actions that came out of
the meeting, each with an ID that the registers refer to. The raw transcript stays in `inbox/`.
