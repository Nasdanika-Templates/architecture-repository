[#ai-risk]
= AI Risk and Governance

_CPMAI Phases V and VI. NIST AI RMF: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage._

[#risk-classification]
== Classification

EU AI Act::
[.proposed]#PROPOSED# A limited-risk system with transparency obligations: customers must be told
that they are interacting with an AI (R-1). The assistant informs and hands over; it does not
evaluate creditworthiness or make any decision about the customer, which would make it high-risk.
The classification is for the AI Governance Board to confirm (<<adr-011>>).

Model risk management::
Whether the assistant is a model under the bank's model risk policy, and so subject to independent
validation, is an open question for Model Risk Management (<<oq-07>>). The design assumes it will
be: the evaluation plan below is written to be the basis of a validation.

[#map]
== Map: Risks

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Risk |OWASP LLM |Mitigation

|A wrong or invented answer about fees, terms or procedures
|LLM09
|Answers only from retrieved passages, with citations; grounding check before sending; refusal and
handover when grounding fails; content owners keep content current (<<a-03>>).

|A customer manipulates the assistant through the prompt
|LLM01
|Prompt shields; system prompt with strict scope; tools that are read-only and scoped to the
customer's token, so a successful injection can read nothing new (<<adr-005>>).

|Instructions hidden in ingested content
|LLM01, LLM08
|Only approved, published content is ingested; ingestion strips active content; content changes
go through the existing publishing approval.

|Personal data leaks into logs, prompts to other customers, or training
|LLM02
|Index the public, fetch the personal (<<data-principles>>); no training on conversations
(<<adr-006>>); message text kept out of telemetry.

|The assistant acts beyond its remit
|LLM06
|No write tools at all in the first release (<<adr-005>>); the tool list is an allow-list.

|The system prompt or internal details are revealed
|LLM07
|Nothing secret in the system prompt; output screening for internal markers.

|Runaway cost or denial of service
|LLM10
|Per-session and per-customer rate limits; token budgets per conversation; quota alerts.

|A vulnerable customer is not recognized
|-
|Sensitive-topic detection with handover (R-5); topics list owned by the Contact Center.
|===

[#evaluation-plan]
== Measure: Evaluation Plan

The golden question set is a few hundred real, anonymized questions, with expected answers and
sources, written and owned by the AI product owner with the Contact Center. It covers every use
case, the sensitive topics, and adversarial prompts from the risks above.

[cols="3,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Metric |Threshold |Measured by

|Groundedness: answers supported by the passages and tool results
|[.assumption]#ASSUMPTION# at least 95% (<<a-08>>)
|Automated groundedness evaluation, calibrated against a human-reviewed sample

|Correctness on the golden set
|[.assumption]#ASSUMPTION# at least 90% (<<a-08>>)
|Human review of a stratified sample

|Sensitive topics handed over
|100% of the sensitive-topic questions in the set
|Automated, from the router's decisions

|Adversarial prompts resisted
|No disclosure of other customers' data; no out-of-scope action
|Red-team set, automated and manual

|Retrieval recall at 5
|Used to choose the embedding model (<<adr-003>>)
|Automated, from the expected sources

|Latency
|R-7
|Load test in pre-production
|===

The suite runs before launch, before every model or prompt change, and monthly on a sample of
production conversations, with the results recorded in the repository as a snapshot
(<<external-information>>).

[#manage]
== Manage: Oversight in Operation

* *Transparency.* The chat says it is an AI, shows the sources of its answers, and offers a human
  at every turn.
* *Human oversight.* Conversation Oversight reviews a sample of conversations each week, and every
  conversation flagged by a customer, a guardrail or an agent.
* *Monitoring.* The metrics in <<operations>>, with thresholds that alert the AI product owner.
* *Incidents.* A wrong answer that harmed a customer is handled as a complaint and as an AI incident:
  root cause in the content, the retrieval, the prompt or the model; a fix; a new case in the golden
  set.
* *Kill switch.* The AI product owner can turn the chat off, leaving the contact center options, in
  minutes and without a release.
