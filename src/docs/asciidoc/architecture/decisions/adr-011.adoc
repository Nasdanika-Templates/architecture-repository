= ADR-011: EU AI Act Classification

Status:: [.pending]#PENDING# 2026-10-30. Proposed by the architects; waits for the AI Governance
Board.

Context:: The bank applies the EU AI Act's classification as its internal standard for AI systems
facing customers. The assistant informs and hands over; it makes no decision about customers.

Alternatives::
* Limited risk, with transparency obligations.
* High risk, if any part of the assistant were used to evaluate customers.

Decision:: Pending. Proposed: limited risk, with the transparency obligations met by R-1 and the
disclosures in the apps.

Consequences:: If confirmed, the obligations are transparency to customers and the bank's own AI
policy. If a feature were later to evaluate customers, such as pre-qualifying them for credit, the
classification would be revisited before that feature is built.

Related:: <<risk-classification>>, <<oq-07>>.
