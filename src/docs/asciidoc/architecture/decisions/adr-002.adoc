Status:: [.proposed]#PROPOSED# 2026-10-30, drafted by the AI assistant and the architects, for the
Architecture Review Board.

Context:: The assistant must answer from approved content and the customer's own data, with the
control over grounding, auditability and data handling that a regulated, customer-facing channel
requires, and within a time frame the sponsor finds acceptable.

Alternatives:: Evaluated in <<solution-patterns>>, with criteria weighted by AHP. Ranking:
{eval-solution-pattern-ranking}.
ifdef::eval-solution-pattern-close[]
The top two are {eval-solution-pattern-margin} apart, a close call.
endif::[]

Decision:: Proposed: {eval-solution-pattern-custom-rag}, on Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Search, with
Azure AI Content Safety and Azure Cosmos DB; {eval-solution-pattern-managed-agent} as the fallback.

Consequences:: The bank builds and operates the Chat Orchestrator (WP-3) and owns its guardrails.
The answer pattern, retrieval plus read-only tools, keeps personal data out of the index and keeps
the existing entitlements in force.
