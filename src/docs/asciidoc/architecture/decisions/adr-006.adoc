= ADR-006: Fine-Tune a Model on Contact-Center Transcripts

Status:: [.rejected]#REJECTED# 2026-10-28, Head of Digital Channels, on the advice of the Data
Protection Officer, at the kick-off (<<meeting-2026-10-28-kickoff>>).

Context:: Contact-center transcripts contain the answers agents actually give. Fine-tuning a model on
them was suggested as a way to make the assistant sound like the bank.

Alternatives::
* Fine-tune on transcripts.
* Retrieval over approved content, with the style set by the system prompt.

Decision:: Rejected. Transcripts contain personal data, and agents' answers are not approved
content: a model trained on them would learn their mistakes and could reproduce personal data.
Retrieval over approved content gives traceable answers that are corrected by correcting the
content.

Consequences:: No customer data is used for training (<<data-principles>>). Transcripts may still be
used, anonymized, to write the golden question set.

Related:: <<adr-002>>.
