= ADR-003: Embedding Model

Status:: [.pending]#PENDING# 2026-10-30. Waits for the retrieval evaluation on the golden question
set, <<poc-1>> (<<oq-06>>). To be decided by the AI product owner.

Context:: The embedding model determines how well the assistant finds the right passages, which
bounds the quality of every answer about products and procedures.

Alternatives:: Evaluated in <<embedding-models>>. Ranking with the drafted scores:
{eval-embedding-models-ranking}.

Decision:: Pending. Replace the drafted retrieval scores of {eval-embedding-models-te3-large} and
{eval-embedding-models-te3-small} with the recall measured by <<poc-1>>, and decide on the new
ranking; if they are
still within the evaluation's close margin, choose the cheaper one.

Consequences:: Changing the embedding model later means re-embedding the content and rebuilding the
index, a small job at this content size.

Related:: <<adr-001>>, <<evaluation-plan>>, <<a-07>>.
