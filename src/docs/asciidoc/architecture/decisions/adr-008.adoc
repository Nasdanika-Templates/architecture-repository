= ADR-008: Voice Channel

Status:: [.deferred]#DEFERRED# 2026-10-28, Head of Digital Channels. Revisit after general
availability of the chat.

Context:: Voice would serve the customers who call the contact center, but adds speech recognition,
speech synthesis, telephony integration and new risks, such as voice spoofing.

Decision:: Deferred. The trigger to revisit: the chat at general availability, with its resolution
rate known.

Related:: <<vision-scope>>.
