[#alternatives]
= Alternatives and Evaluation

_ADM Phase E. CPMAI Phase IV, Model Development._

Three choices shape the solution: the solution pattern, the embedding model and the chat model. For
each, the assistant identified the alternatives, proposed criteria and weights, scored each
alternative against each criterion with a reason, and proposed what-if scenarios. People reviewed
the criteria, the weights and the reasons. The evaluations are data, in `src/evaluations/`; the
tables below are generated from them by the build, and the decisions are bound to their results
(<<evaluations>>). The model scores are drafts, to be replaced by measurements
(<<evaluation-plan>>).

[IMPORTANT]
====
A ranking is an input to a decision, not the decision (<<ai-drafts-people-decide>>). Where two
alternatives are close, the ranking says that they are close, not which one is better.
====

[.assumption]#ASSUMPTION# Product and model names, capabilities and regional availability are as
understood when this was drafted and must be checked against the current Azure catalog before a
decision (<<a-07>>).

[#solution-patterns]
== Solution Pattern

The criteria are weighted with the Analytic Hierarchy Process, from pairwise comparisons agreed
with the Architecture Review Board's delegates (<<ahp>>).

include::{evaluations-dir}/solution-pattern.adoc[]

Interpretation::
{eval-solution-pattern-custom-rag} gives the bank full control over every step, which scores well
with Security, Model Risk and Compliance, at the price of building and operating more.
{eval-solution-pattern-managed-agent} keeps most of that control inside the bank's tenant and is
faster to deliver. [.assumption]#ASSUMPTION# It is approved by the bank's cloud governance for
customer data (<<oq-08>>).
ifeval::["{eval-solution-pattern-scenario-speed-first-id}" != "{eval-solution-pattern-first-id}"]
The ranking is sensitive to how speed is weighed against control: in the scenario
_{eval-solution-pattern-scenario-speed}_, {eval-solution-pattern-scenario-speed-first} ranks first.
endif::[]

Recommendation::
{eval-solution-pattern-custom-rag}, with {eval-solution-pattern-managed-agent} as the fallback if
the bank's cloud governance approves it and the sponsor's deadline requires it (<<adr-002>>,
[.proposed]#PROPOSED#).

[#embedding-models]
== Embedding Model

include::{evaluations-dir}/embedding-models.adoc[]

Interpretation::
The content is in English and small, a few thousand pages, so cost and index size matter little in
absolute terms, and the difference in retrieval quality on _this_ content is unknown.
{eval-embedding-models-te3-large} can also be used with reduced dimensions, which narrows its
disadvantage in index size. {eval-embedding-models-cohere} would need an exception to <<adr-001>>;
in the scenario _{eval-embedding-models-scenario-multilingual}_ the ranking is
{eval-embedding-models-scenario-multilingual-ranking}.

Recommendation::
Measure {eval-embedding-models-te3-large} and {eval-embedding-models-te3-small} on the golden
question set, recall at 5 and at 10, replace their retrieval scores with the results, and decide on
the new ranking (<<adr-003>>, [.pending]#PENDING# <<oq-06>>).

[#chat-models]
== Chat Model

include::{evaluations-dir}/chat-models.adoc[]

Interpretation::
The ranking turns on how groundedness is weighed against cost. For a regulated bank that is not an
academic variation: the cost of a wrong answer may well exceed the cost of the tokens. In the
scenario _{eval-chat-models-scenario-groundedness-first}_, {eval-chat-models-scenario-groundedness-first-first}
ranks first.

Routing::
The choice need not be exclusive. A common design answers with a smaller model and routes complex
or low-confidence questions to a larger one. It adds a component and an evaluation per route; it is
worth it only if no single model meets both the quality and the latency thresholds.

Recommendation::
Evaluate {eval-chat-models-gpt-41}, {eval-chat-models-gpt-41-mini} and
{eval-chat-models-gpt-5-mini} on the golden question set against the thresholds in
<<evaluation-plan>>, update their scores, and choose the cheapest model that meets all the
thresholds (<<adr-004>>, [.pending]#PENDING# <<oq-06>>).
