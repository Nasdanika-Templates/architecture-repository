[#vision]
= Architecture Vision

_ADM Phase A. CPMAI Phase I, Business Understanding._

[#vision-problem]
== Problem

[.fact]#FACT# The contact center of {bank} answers a high volume of routine questions from personal
banking customers: fees, card and account procedures, the meaning of transactions, opening hours,
how to use the {system}. Many of these questions are answered in the bank's published help content,
which customers do not find or do not read (<<meeting-2026-10-28-kickoff>>).

[.assumption]#ASSUMPTION# A large share of routine contacts could be resolved by a conversational
assistant that can also see the customer's own recent transactions (<<a-01>>).

[#vision-ai-fit]
== Is AI the Right Solution?

CPMAI asks this before anything else. The answer drafted here is a qualified yes:

* The questions are in natural language and varied, which rules-based chatbots handle poorly.
* The answers exist, in the bank's approved content and the customer's own data. The assistant
  retrieves and explains them; it does not need to create knowledge, which keeps the risk of wrong
  answers manageable (<<adr-002>>).
* The cost of a wrong answer is bounded by the scope: no payments, no advice, read-only data, and a
  human one click away (<<adr-005>>).

A better search on the help pages is the non-AI alternative. It would serve the product questions
but not "why was I charged this fee?", which needs the customer's own data.

[#vision-scope]
== Scope

In scope::
* The chat in the single-page application and the mobile app, for signed-in personal banking
  customers, in English.
* Answers from the bank's approved help and product content.
* Read-only access to the customer's own accounts and recent transactions, through the existing API
  application.
* Handover to a human agent in the contact center, with the conversation so far.

Out of scope for the first release::
* Payments, transfers or any other change from the chat (<<adr-005>>).
* Investment, credit or tax advice.
* Voice (<<adr-008>>).
* Languages other than English (<<oq-05>>).

[#vision-constraints]
== Constraints

[.fact]#FACT# These constraints were stated at the kick-off (<<meeting-2026-10-28-kickoff>>):

* Language models run on Azure OpenAI in the bank's own Azure tenant (<<adr-001>>).
* Customer data stays in the bank's Azure regions; all traffic uses private networking.
* The API application of the {system} is the only path to the Mainframe Banking System.
* The bank's AI governance and model risk processes apply before any customer sees the chat.
