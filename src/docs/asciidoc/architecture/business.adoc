[#business]
= Business Architecture

_ADM Phase B._

[#capabilities]
== Capabilities

[cols="2,3,1"]
|===
|Capability |Description |Change

|Customer Self-Service
|Customers find information and complete tasks without a human.
|Extended

|Conversational Assistance
|Answering customers' questions in natural language, from approved content and their own data.
|New

|Assisted Service Handover
|Moving a conversation from the assistant to a human agent with its context.
|New

|Content Management
|Authoring, approving and publishing help and product content.
|Extended: content becomes an input to the assistant, so its quality matters more

|Conversation Oversight
|Reviewing conversations for quality, conduct and safety; handling complaints about the assistant.
|New
|===

[#use-cases]
== Use Cases

[cols="1,3,3"]
|===
|ID |Use case |Example

|UC-1
|Ask about products, fees and procedures
|"How do I order a replacement card?" "What is the fee for an international transfer?"

|UC-2
|Ask about one's own accounts and transactions
|"Why was I charged $15 on 2 October?" "When did my salary arrive?"

|UC-3
|Get help using the {system}
|"Where do I download a statement?"

|UC-4
|Be handed over to a human
|On request, when the assistant cannot answer, or when the topic is sensitive: bereavement,
fraud, financial difficulty, complaints.

|UC-5
|Review conversations
|Conversation Oversight samples conversations and reviews flagged ones.
|===

[#requirements]
== Requirements

[cols="1,5"]
|===
|ID |Requirement

|R-1
|Customers are told, before the first message, that they are talking to an AI assistant, and can
ask for a human at any time.

|R-2
|Answers about products and procedures are based only on approved, published content, and cite it.

|R-3
|Answers about accounts use only the signed-in customer's own data, retrieved at the time of the
question, with the customer's existing permissions.

|R-4
|The assistant does not initiate payments, change data, or give investment, credit or tax advice.

|R-5
|Sensitive topics, as defined by the Contact Center and Compliance, are handed over to a human.

|R-6
|Conversations are retained and can be retrieved for complaints and audits, under the bank's
records policy (<<oq-04>>).

|R-7
|The first response token arrives within [.assumption]#ASSUMPTION# 2 seconds for 95% of messages
(<<a-06>>).
|===
