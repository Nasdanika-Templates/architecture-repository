== 2026-10-28: Kick-off

Source:: `inbox/2026-10-28-kickoff-transcript.txt`, a Microsoft Teams transcript, reviewed.

Participants:: Head of Digital Channels (sponsor, chair), Contact Center Lead, Enterprise Architect,
Information Security Lead, {system} Product Owner, Data Protection Officer, Model Risk Manager.

Summary:: The meeting set the scope of the {feature}: a read-only chat for signed-in customers,
inside the existing web and mobile apps, answering from approved content and the customer's own
data, on Azure OpenAI, with handover to the contact center. It rejected fine-tuning on transcripts,
deferred voice, and asked the architects for evaluated alternatives for the solution pattern and
the models.

Facts::
* Most answers to routine questions are already in the bank's help content; customers do not find
  them. (Head of Digital Channels)
* Azure OpenAI in the bank's tenant is the approved source of language models; others need an
  exception. (Enterprise Architect)
* Customer data stays in the bank's regions, behind private endpoints: the landing zone standard.
  (Information Security Lead)

Decisions::
* Azure OpenAI is the source of language models: <<adr-001>>. (Head of Digital Channels, confirming
  the cloud strategy)
* The chat is part of the web and mobile apps, for signed-in customers; the standalone chat app of
  the pre-study is dropped: <<adr-010>>, superseding <<adr-009>>. (Head of Digital Channels)
* The first release is read-only: <<adr-005>>. (Head of Digital Channels)
* No fine-tuning on contact-center transcripts: <<adr-006>>. (Head of Digital Channels, on the DPO's
  advice)
* Voice after general availability: <<adr-008>>. (Head of Digital Channels)

Assumptions::
* <<a-01>>: a large share of routine contacts could be resolved by the assistant. The Contact Center
  Lead could not give the split by topic.
* <<a-04>>: the contact center platform accepts chats from an external channel; to be confirmed
  with the vendor.

Open questions::
* <<oq-04>>: retention of conversations. (Data Protection Officer)
* <<oq-05>>: languages besides English, and when. (Head of Digital Channels)
* <<oq-07>>: whether the assistant is a model under the model risk policy. (Model Risk Manager)

Actions::
* Classify a month of contacts by topic. Contact Center Lead. No date stated.
* Lay out the alternatives, with criteria, for the solution pattern and the models, and bring a
  recommendation to the Architecture Review Board. Architects. Before the next meeting.
* Next meeting in two weeks.
